Buying books

You need an account to buy books. Visitors can browse, search, read reviews and chat, but only registered customers and VIP customers can buy. You pay from your account balance; add money on the My account page. All prices are in US dollars.

Returns

All sales are final: books cannot be returned or refunded.

Not enough money and fines

If you try to buy a book that costs more than your balance, the purchase is refused and a $1 fine is taken from your balance. If the fine makes your balance negative, your account is suspended. A suspended account cannot log in until the store administrator reopens it, which needs a positive balance.

VIP customers

A registered customer becomes a VIP customer after more than 3 purchases or after spending more than $200. VIP customers get 10% off every book.

Reviews and comments

Anyone can rate a book from 1 to 5 stars and leave a comment. Ratings from customers who bought the book count double in the book's average. Words on the store's taboo list are replaced with asterisks. A comment with more than 3 taboo words is blocked and its author gets a warning. A registered customer with more than 2 warnings is suspended, and a VIP customer who gets a warning goes back to being a registered customer.

Ask the Book

On a book's page you can ask questions about the book, answered from its full text. Visitors can try 3 questions; registered customers can ask as many as they like.
